IRAQ • 15 OCTOBER
Saddam Hussein's referendum
Iraq held a presidential referendum on 15 October 2002. The vote took place while international attention was focused on the possibility of a future US-led war against Iraq.
INDONESIA • BALI
Bali bombing investigation
The 12 October Bali bombing remained a major international story. Indonesian authorities were investigating the attack while international pressure for identification of those responsible increased.
SAUDI ARABIA / SUDAN
Hijacking attempt stopped
A passenger attempted to hijack a Saudi Arabian Airlines aircraft shortly after it departed Khartoum. Crew members overcame the passenger and he was arrested.
KOREA • ASIAN GAMES
North Korean athletes in South Korea
North Korean athletes were competing in South Korea during the 2002 Asian Games, giving the sporting event an unusual inter-Korean dimension.
INDIA • JAMMU & KASHMIR
Government formation negotiations
Following the Jammu and Kashmir Assembly election, Congress and the People's Democratic Party were negotiating the formation of a government.
TAMIL NADU • CAUVERY
Cauvery water dispute
On this date, DMK president M. Karunanidhi called for the Centre to invoke Article 256 over Karnataka's compliance with directions concerning Cauvery water.
INDIA • 15 OCTOBER
A. P. J. Abdul Kalam's birthday
President A. P. J. Abdul Kalam turned 72 on 15 October 2002. Tamil Nadu Chief Minister Jayalalithaa and DMK leader M. Karunanidhi were among those who sent birthday greetings.
MICROSOFT • TECHNOLOGY
Bluetooth desktop hardware
Microsoft announced retail availability of a wireless optical desktop solution using Bluetooth technology.

Rishivandiyam — 2002 Assembly Seat

In the 2001 Tamil Nadu Assembly election, S. Sivaraj of Tamil Maanila Congress (Moopanar) won Rishivandiyam.

Votes: 57,108

Constituency number at the time: 71

Important Parliamentary Boundary Note

Today's Rishivandiyam Assembly constituency belongs to the Kallakurichi Lok Sabha constituency.

But the Kallakurichi Lok Sabha constituency was created only after the 2008 delimitation.

Therefore the current MP must NOT be presented as the MP who represented this exact parliamentary configuration in 2002.

Today's Boundary

Rishivandiyam is now Assembly Constituency No. 78 and forms part of the Kallakurichi parliamentary constituency.

2002 BOUNDARY ≠ 2026 BOUNDARY

10 • BEAUTY & FASHION

Beauty & Pageant Era

The early-2000s beauty aesthetic: glossy lips, kajal, straightened hair, sarees and salwar styles.

💄 Beauty Look

Kajal-lined eyes, glossy lips, warm-toned makeup, thin-to-medium brows and sleek hairstyles were common early-2000s visual references.

👗 Fashion

Sarees, salwar kameez, churidars and Indo-western silhouettes were prominent in Indian fashion.

👑 Pageant Era

India remained strongly associated with international beauty-pageant culture in the late-1990s and early-2000s.

Miss World 2002 was ultimately won by Azra Akın of Turkey in December 2002.

Technology on 15 October 2002

The bridge between the PC era and the coming mobile revolution.

🔵 Bluetooth

Microsoft announced retail availability of a wireless optical desktop solution using Bluetooth.

🍎 Apple

Apple-related technology coverage included discussion of Mac OS X's upcoming journaling file system and PowerPC developments.

📶 Wi-Fi

802.11b wireless networking and efforts toward Wi-Fi/3G interoperability were part of the technology landscape.

📱 Mobile Phones

Feature phones dominated. Text messaging and voice calls were central; app stores and smartphones were still years away from becoming mainstream.

💾 Storage

CDs, floppy disks, hard drives and USB storage were normal consumer technologies.

🌐 The Web

Search engines, portals, forums, email and personal websites defined the web experience.
